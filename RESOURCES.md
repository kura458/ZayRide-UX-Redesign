\## 6. AI Assistance Disclosure



ChatGPT was used to assist with organizing project documentation, explaining Android XML concepts, suggesting UI/UX improvements, and troubleshooting development issues.



The student is responsible for reviewing the generated content, inspecting the reference materials, verifying the sources, and understanding the final design decisions.



AI assistance was used as a supporting learning tool and not as a replacement for the student's own analysis and work.



\## 7. Limitations



The original ZayRide Passenger application was not installed or tested directly. The evaluation uses publicly available information and archived screenshots.



The Android prototype is a static interface redesign and does not connect to ZayRide's live backend services.
